# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in OpenGuardrail a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level or role in the community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific in technical discussions.
- Assume good intent while addressing the observable impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate and learn from mistakes.
- Keep security reports, personal information and private operational data out of
  public issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized language and imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, vulnerabilities or private infrastructure.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  open-source community.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review discussions,
community events and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

The project uses GitHub's built-in reporting tools in
[alibaba/open-guardrail](https://github.com/alibaba/open-guardrail) for its initial
public release. It does not use a separate conduct-reporting mailbox.

- For disruptive content in an issue, pull request or comment, open its **...**
  menu, choose **Report content**, select a reason and choose **Report to
  repository admins**. Repository administrators review reports through GitHub's
  reported-content tools.
- GitHub restricts access to repository reporting based on the reporter's
  relationship to the repository. If the administrator-reporting option is
  unavailable, or a report concerns a repository administrator, use
  [GitHub's abuse-reporting procedure](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
  to report to GitHub Support. GitHub Support handles violations of GitHub's
  policies; it is a separate recipient and does not serve as the project's
  confidential inbox.
- For project-hosted spaces outside GitHub, use the host's moderation tools or
  contact its designated moderator privately. Organizers must publish that route
  before opening a chat or event.

Use these reporting tools rather than posting incident details in a new public
issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Where the reporting form allows details, include what happened, when and where,
supporting links and a safe way to reply. Share only the details needed to
investigate; redact unrelated personal data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports they receive confidentially, share information only as
needed to investigate and address the incident, and recuse themselves from
decisions where they have a conflict of interest. They may edit or remove content,
issue a warning, restrict participation or permanently ban a participant when
necessary.
Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and focused on
protecting the community.
